The 'Why' behind this site

As the family expand, I want a good way to place the history of us with our most important photos and stories.

This site will have pages for Rob and Sherry and each of their kids. The most important photos, and the stories behind those photos. There are so many family stories that shouldn't be lost. It is my hope that these pages will also contain a method for each of us to add new stories for our family to remember.

This is a great example. While this photo was taken decades before I was born, I never knew my grandparents. It is only through a DNA test and great new cousins that I now have this image.
