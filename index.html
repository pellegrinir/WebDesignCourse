How it all began

Rob is from Connecticut and Sherry from California. So they met in the most "likely" of all places, in Argentina, at a funeral.


They only saw each other four or five times throughout the next year and a half. But once each had returned home, Rob called her while he was visiting California. And the rest... The rest is history!
